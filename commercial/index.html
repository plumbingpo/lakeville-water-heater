Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Lakeville Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Lakeville and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and every response is fully documented for your facility files.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your property's specific equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include anode checks and tank flushing, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve service, and temperature readings at key fixtures. Mineral-heavy water cuts the life of commercial equipment fast, so busy properties usually benefit from quarterly visits.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
